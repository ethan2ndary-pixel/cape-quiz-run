Cape Mountains Quiz Runner

Created by Ethan

How to play

  • Tap / click the screen or press Space to jump.
  • Collect glowing orbs — some orbs trigger quiz questions. Answer to continue.
  • Avoid spikes — hitting a spike ends your run.
  • Correct answers give bonus points. Try to beat your high score!
